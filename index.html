01 — Who I am

Engineering, end to end.

MF
MO UHCINE · FADOUL
Flutter Dev CCNA 1–4 App Architect

My road didn't start with a screen — it started with a switch and a router. I trained as a specialized network technician (NTIC II) and earned the full Cisco CCNA suite (1–4), configuring networks, hardening firewalls, and administering Windows & Linux systems. That discipline still shapes how I build software.

Curiosity pulled me into Flutter & Dart, where I taught myself to ship. The proof is Muslim Platform — a complete Islamic companion app that runs entirely offline, collects zero data, and serves users across the world from Casablanca.

  • Networking mindset — CCNA-grade rigor applied to architecture & resilience.
  • Privacy as a feature — every calculation happens on-device, always.
  • From wire to widget — comfortable configuring L2/L3, and painting beautiful UIs.
