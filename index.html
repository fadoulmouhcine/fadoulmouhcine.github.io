About me

I'm Mouhcine Fadoul, a mobile app developer from Casablanca, Morocco. My journey started in computer networking — I trained as a specialized network technician and earned Cisco CCNA 1–4 certifications, configuring switches and routers, managing Windows & Linux systems and troubleshooting hardware and networks.

Driven by curiosity, I taught myself Flutter & Dart and built Muslim Platform, a complete offline-first Islamic app for Android — private by design, with every prayer time and Qibla calculation running on-device.

Featured project

Main project · Android

Muslim Platform

A free, offline-first Islamic companion app. All content and calculations live on the device — no accounts, no ads, no tracking.

  • Prayer times & Adhan notifications
  • Qibla direction
  • Quran reader with audio recitation (multiple reciters)
  • Hadith library — Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim, Tirmidhi
  • Tasbih, athkar & ad'iyah
  • Hassad (daily harvest) tracker & Friday hub
  • Nearby mosques & Hijri calendar
